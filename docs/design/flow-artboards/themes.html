Graph themes

The trip planner's real flow, read from planner.py, drawn five ways. Each one keeps the same meanings: the dot is health (teal up, amber needs a look, coral down), the thick ink line is the critical path, and an italic weight is an estimate rather than a measurement. Pick the ones worth building; the map gets a theme menu.

Studio

Today's cards, refined: a health edge on each card and the timing under it.

  • Most familiar
  • Densest information

Boxed

Blocks sit in containers of what runs together, read from the dependency graph. The boxes are the advice.

  • Shows parallel work at a glance
  • Labels say how long each box takes

Blueprint

An engineering drawing: outlines on a grid, and each timing drawn as a dimension line under its block.

  • Calm and technical
  • Timing is part of the drawing

Metro

A transit map: each dependency is a station, line colours are kinds, and the critical path is the main line.

  • The most distinctive
  • Least room for detail; details open on click

Soft

Calm colour fields by kind, no borders, branches as dotted lines.

  • Friendliest for screenshots and demos
  • Kind colours carry the structure
